BN14 Studio: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

BN14 Studio is a private, single-user desktop tool. It is operated by the owner of the BN14 Unlocked social media accounts (Instagram @bn14.unlocked, TikTok @bn14_unlocked, YouTube "BN14 Unlocked") to publish the owner's own edited short videos to the owner's own accounts. It is not offered to other people.

What data the app uses

  • Account authorization tokens issued by TikTok, Google/YouTube and Meta when the owner connects their own account. They are used only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's account and to read basic profile information (account name and ID) needed to do so.
  • The owner's video files, titles and captions, which the owner creates and approves before each upload.

What the app does not do

  • It does not access, collect or store data about any other user.
  • It does not read followers, messages, comments or analytics of other people.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use data for advertising or profiling.

Storage and security

Tokens and files are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local folder that is not synced or shared. Tokens are never logged, displayed or sent anywhere except to the platform that issued them. The owner can revoke access at any time in the platform's account settings (TikTok: Settings > Security > Manage app permissions; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions; Meta: Settings > Apps and websites). After revocation the app can no longer act on the account, and the owner can delete the local folder to remove all stored data.

YouTube API Services

BN14 Studio uses the YouTube API Services to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (videos.insert) and to show the connected channel name (channels.list). By using BN14 Studio you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy). BN14 Studio stores only the owner's Google access and refresh tokens on the owner's computer; it does not store any YouTube data other than the IDs of the videos it uploaded. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page (https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions); the stored tokens are then useless and are deleted from the local folder when the owner removes it or reconnects.

Data retention

Local files are kept only as long as the owner needs them and can be deleted at any time.

Children

The app is not directed to children and is used only by its adult owner.
